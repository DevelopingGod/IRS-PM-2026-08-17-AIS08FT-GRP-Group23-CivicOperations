# NYC311 complaint families and responsible agencies

This matrix expands every agency abbreviation used in the project summary. The
responsible agency can change after subtype selection or agency triage.

| Complaint or request family | Responsible agency (full name and abbreviation) |
|---|---|
| Apartment maintenance; heat or hot water; mold, pests, leaks, plumbing, or electrical conditions in private residential buildings | New York City Department of Housing Preservation and Development (**HPD**) |
| Public-housing maintenance | New York City Housing Authority (**NYCHA**) |
| Illegal construction, unsafe buildings, façades, scaffolds, cranes, excavations, elevators, or escalators | New York City Department of Buildings (**DOB**) |
| Illegal parking, blocked driveways, neighbor/street/vehicle noise | New York City Police Department (**NYPD**) |
| Abandoned vehicles | New York City Department of Sanitation (**DSNY**) or New York City Police Department (**NYPD**), depending on plates and location |
| Construction, machinery, commercial-equipment, air, smoke, fumes, or odor complaints | New York City Department of Environmental Protection (**DEP**) |
| Bar, restaurant, or business noise | New York City Police Department (**NYPD**) and/or New York City Department of Environmental Protection (**DEP**), depending on subtype |
| Potholes, street defects, sidewalks, streetlights, signals, signs, or transportation infrastructure | New York City Department of Transportation (**DOT**) |
| Blocked street or sidewalk | New York City Department of Transportation (**DOT**), New York City Department of Sanitation (**DSNY**), New York City Department of Buildings (**DOB**), or New York City Police Department (**NYPD**), depending on cause |
| Missed collection, dirty sidewalk/street, illegal dumping, trash storage, snow or ice | New York City Department of Sanitation (**DSNY**) |
| Street water leaks, sewers, catch basins, flooding, or water-quality/pressure complaints | New York City Department of Environmental Protection (**DEP**) |
| Restaurant sanitation, food poisoning, rodents, mosquitoes, standing water, smoking or vaping | New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (**DOHMH**) |
| Animal facility, nuisance, or stray-animal complaints | New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (**DOHMH**), New York City Police Department (**NYPD**), or Animal Care Centers of NYC (**ACC**), depending on risk and subtype |
| Street trees, fallen branches, parks, or playground maintenance | New York City Department of Parks and Recreation (**DPR**, commonly “NYC Parks”) |
| Consumer, store, pricing, receipt, refund, scale, deceptive-practice, or workplace complaints | New York City Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (**DCWP**) |
| Taxi, car-service, or for-hire vehicle complaints and lost property | New York City Taxi and Limousine Commission (**TLC**) |
| Homeless outreach or shelter complaints | New York City Department of Homeless Services (**DHS**) |
| Public-school complaints | New York City Department of Education (**DOE**) |
| Child-care and child-welfare complaints | New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (**DOHMH**) or New York City Administration for Children’s Services (**ACS**) |
| Benefits, SNAP, Medicaid, or cash-assistance complaints | New York City Department of Social Services / Human Resources Administration (**DSS/HRA**) |
| Discrimination complaints | New York City Commission on Human Rights (**CCHR**) |
| Parking tickets, property tax, or City payment issues | New York City Department of Finance (**DOF**) |
| Fire-safety and fire-code complaints | New York City Fire Department (**FDNY**); active emergencies go to **911** |
